The service

Quizzy McQuizface lets a host start a live trivia quiz and invite other players to join with a code. Quiz sessions are temporary: they exist only while players are actively playing and are not saved once a quiz ends or the server restarts.

User-generated content

Display names and round topics are entered by you and other players, and are shown to everyone in the same session. You agree not to submit names or topics that are offensive, harassing, illegal, or that impersonate another person.

All submitted names and topics pass through automated content moderation before being shown to other players. Every player also has access to in-app Report and Remove controls to flag or remove another player from a lobby they host. We may remove content or players at our discretion to keep the app safe to use.
